# Board meeting: OliveIQ

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, 0 FUND, 0 PASS. Average score 4.3 out of 10.**

| Lens | Score | Vote |

All three would back it, but only if the conditions below are met. The scores
are low because the evidence is missing, not because the idea is bad. Nobody
has user, conversion, acquisition-cost or competitor numbers yet. The Monopoly
lens says it moves to PASS if those numbers do not come back.

## Risks more than one member raised (most dangerous first)

1. **No distribution (all three).** There is no channel yet. The buyers are
   older, rural and scattered, and the cost to win each one is unknown and
   must be very low at €49 a year. Offers and Monopoly both point to the same
   fix: sell through mills, cooperatives or accountants who reach many growers
   at once, not one grove at a time.
2. **Value arrives too late (Offers, Product).** A logbook asks for data entry
   all season before it pays back. The profit and cost-per-litre views are
   empty for weeks. The founder notes there is no evidence yet that retention
   has improved.
3. **Too many features and no single promise (Offers, Product).** The app has
   about ten features. The pitch adds satellite, irrigation and traceability.
   The paid tier is a feature list, not an outcome. Buyers then compare it
   with free options on price.
4. **Unproven core claims (Offers, Product).** Nobody has shown the δάκος
   model matches real trap counts on real groves. Nobody has shown an
   accountant or OPEKEPE accepting the PDF.
5. **The mill tier was cut (Monopoly, raised by Offers as a question).** That
   tier was the one route to a cheap channel and to data that copycats cannot
   replicate.

## Where they disagree

- **What the moat is.** Monopoly says nothing proprietary exists today. Every
  part could be copied in six months, so only mill data, grove history or
  official compliance status could protect it. Product cares less about
  defensibility and more about polish and the first session.
- **Who to sell to.** Product says narrow the app to one thing for the family
  grower. Offers and Monopoly question whether the family grower is the right
  buyer at all. They point to commercial growers, accountants or mills, who
  have more money and reach.
- **Price.** Offers asks whether €99 would work with a guarantee. The other
  two did not challenge €49.

## Conditions checklist

The rest of the founder pack tests most of these.

- [ ] **Pick one buyer:** family grower, commercial grower, accountant or mill. Test direct sales against selling through a mill, cooperative or accountant. (`/founder-consumer`, `/founder-competitors`)
- [ ] **Pick a beachhead:** one region, or a handful of mills or cooperatives, where a large share is reachable within a year. (`/founder-competitors`)
- [ ] **Get one mill or cooperative to commit** to onboarding its growers. Decide whether to bring back a mill or B2B2C tier. (`/founder-launch`)
- [ ] **Pick the one thing and say it in one Greek sentence:** the δάκος alert or the paperwork-ready logbook. Put satellite, irrigation and traceability on hold, and hide everything else during onboarding. (`/founder-marketing`)
- [ ] **Rebuild Pro around one named outcome with a guarantee the CFO approves,** for example "never miss the δάκος window, ΟΣΔΕ paperwork done". (`/founder-offer`, `/founder-cfo`)
- [ ] **Test the price:** €49 a year against a higher price backed by a guarantee. (`/founder-pricing`)
- [ ] **Give value on day one:** an alert set up, or a logbook started from a template with a sample PDF. Measure retention before and after the change.
- [ ] **One season of proof on 10 to 20 real groves:** compare alerts with trap counts, and confirm the PDF is accepted.
- [ ] **Written confirmation from a few accountants** that the PDF works for ΟΣΔΕ. (`/founder-launch`)
- [ ] **Close the seams:** one name (OliveIQ, not OliveLog or ΕλαιοLog), a live oliveiq.gr, and a guided step to install the app and allow notifications, including the iPhone home-screen step. (`/founder-brand`)
- [ ] **Bring back the numbers:** competitor map, cost to win a customer, free-to-paid conversion, weekly active growers, and push opt-in kept through the season. (`/founder-competitors`, `/founder-cfo`, live analytics)
- [ ] **Name a moat on the roadmap:** accumulated grove history, mill-to-grower links, or official compliance status.

## The strongest version the board can see

This differs from the pitch. The pitch is a farm-management suite sold grove by
grove to family growers. The board sees a stronger business in **the δάκος
alert and the paperwork-ready logbook, sold through the mills and cooperatives
(or the accountants who file ΟΣΔΕ) that each serve dozens of growers.** The
mill gets a branded tool and cleaner delivery records. The grower gets an alert
that matters and paperwork done, with the mill or accountant entering much of
the data. Each grove's history becomes harder to leave every year. That tackles
all three lenses' biggest worries at once: distribution, slow value and no
moat. Before it gets near a FUND, one season on 10 to 20 real groves needs to
prove the alert works and the PDF is accepted.
