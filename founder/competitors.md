## Urgent: a trademark conflict with the name

A USPTO record shows **"OLIV IQ" as a registered trademark owned by Boundary
Bend IP Pty Ltd, covering olive grove software, measuring instruments and
agricultural services**
([gleanmark](https://gleanmark.com/trademark/uspto-79274997)). Boundary Bend
is a large olive company. The record looks like an international
registration (Madrid system). Check whether it covers the EU or Greece before
spending anything on the OliveIQ brand. `/founder-brand` should treat this as
a blocker.

## 3. What their customers complain about

**Thin. There were too few public reviews to count themes.** The only ratings
found were 3 for CropApp on the App Store and 8 that OliveSuite reports on its
own page. The strongest signal is not a review but an industry report:

- **Growers do not keep their own diary.** The ΚΥΔ say many producers cannot
  make the entries without their consultant
  ([agrotypos](https://www.agrotypos.gr/chrima/opekepe-ypaat/eae-2026-ekkremotites-me-tis-psifiakes-efarmoges-kai-ta-oikologika-schimata)).
  One source, and an industry body rather than growers, so treat it as thin.
- **Agronomists are overloaded at filing time.** The geotechnical chamber
  (ΓΕΩΤΕΕ) asked for an extension because consultants could not issue the
  eco-scheme paperwork in time
  ([agrotypos](https://www.agrotypos.gr/geotee-paratasi-gia-parastatika-sto-oikologiko-schima-psifiakis-efarmogis-kai-allagi-thesmikou-plaisiou-gia-neous-agrotes)).
  One source, thin.

them as proven.

## 4. Positioning map

Two axes matter most to this buyer:

- **Who does the work:** the grower types it in (left) or an advisor or system
  does it (right).
- **How olive-specific it is:** olive-only (top) or generic multi-crop
  (bottom).

```
                 OLIVE-SPECIFIC
                       |
   OliveSuite  OliveIQ |  gaiasense/gaiaTRAP
   Olivio              |  OLIWES (technicians)
                       |  ΔΑΟΚ δακοκτονία
 GROWER DOES IT -------+------- ADVISOR / SYSTEM DOES IT
                       |
   Praktifarm          |  CropApp (via input supplier)
   AgroApps 360        |  Agronomist using a certified 31.6 app
   paper notebook      |  Farmonaut
                       |
                   GENERIC
```

OliveIQ sits in the most crowded and cheapest corner: an olive app the grower
fills in themselves. OliveSuite, its closest twin, charges 25% less and runs on
ads. The money and the alerts sit on the right side of the map, with the
advisors, the trap networks and the state.

## 5. The gap

The evidence points to one gap. It is narrower than the current pitch.

**Nobody seen here builds a simple, olive-only tool for the agronomist, ΚΥΔ or
cooperative who already keeps the diary for dozens of small growers.** The
evidence for that:

- The diary is **required** for the eco-scheme (Π1-31.6-Α needs a certified
  digital app plus a plan from an agronomist,
  [ganaptixi](https://ganaptixi.gr/schetika/schetika-me-dimosiefsi/oikologika-schimata-p1-31-6-enischysi-paragogon-gia-tin-efarmogi-filikon-gia-to-perivallon-praktikon-diacheirisis-me-ti-chrisi-psifiakis-efarmogis-diacheirisis-eisroon-kai-parakolouthisis-perival/)).
  For growers it is not optional.
- In practice the **consultant** does the entries, and consultants are
  overloaded (the two thin sources above).
- The existing certified tools are either multi-crop (CropApp) or built around
  hardware and stations (gaiasense).

That matches the board's strongest version: sell through the people who
already serve many growers. It also exposes a condition the board did not
know about. **To win here, OliveIQ almost certainly needs certification as a
31.6 digital application.** The skill could not find the certification
process. Ask ΟΠΕΚΕΠΕ or ΥΠΑΑΤ, the ministry of agriculture.

The δάκος alert on its own is **not** a gap. gaiasense has stations, traps and
AI counting. The state runs trap networks and sprays. CropApp and OliveSuite
already send pest warnings. A degree-day model from weather data is the
weakest of these.

## 6. The threat: who could copy OliveIQ fastest

1. **OliveSuite.** It already has the same olive-only, grower-facing product,
   a lower price and a free tier with ads. Adding a PDF export or an AI
   assistant would take it weeks.
2. **NEUROPUBLIC (gaiasense).** It has the station and trap network, eco-scheme
   certification, cooperative relationships and the brand. A lightweight
   grower diary app would cost it little, and it already sells to agronomists
   and cooperatives.
3. **AgroApps (CropApp).** It is certified for 31.6, sold through an input
   supplier, and could add an olive mode with δάκος and mill deliveries.

## What this changes for the board's checklist

- **Moat (Monopoly lens):** confirmed weak. Every feature exists somewhere
  already.
- **Channel:** the evidence favours agronomists, ΚΥΔ and cooperatives over
  selling directly to growers.
- **New condition:** find out how to get **eco-scheme 31.6 certification** and
  what it costs.
- **New blocker:** clear the **OLIV IQ trademark** before investing in the
  brand.
- **Price:** €49 is above the comparable grower apps (about €19–37). Without a
  feature they lack, `/founder-pricing` should test whether it holds.
