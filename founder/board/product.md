# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- A real, recurring moment: the grower who has to hand a field logbook to the accountant or OPEKEPE. A PDF that is "ready" for that moment is a concrete result the customer can feel.
- The δάκος alert is the right kind of feature: it reaches the grower at the moment it matters, without them opening the app.
- The founder is already cutting. Collapsing PRODUCER and MILL into one paid tier, and stripping grove setup down to a name and a location, show the habit of saying no.
- The craft is there in places nobody sees: a rules fallback when the AI is off, a degree-day model rather than a generic weather warning, and a funnel that is instrumented.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** Logbook, map, costs, harvests, mill deliveries, profit view, pest alerts, PDF, weather, AI agronomist, task calendar. The draft pitch adds satellite, irrigation and tree-to-shelf traceability. That is a farm-management suite built by one person. A 200-tree family grower will not learn ten features, and a solo builder cannot polish ten. Today the pitch needs a paragraph.
2. **The first five minutes ask for work before they give anything back.** A logbook is only worth something after weeks of entries, and the profit and cost-per-litre views are empty until then. The founder says there is no evidence yet that retention has improved. If the grower does not get something useful in the first session, they will not come back to log the second spray.
3. **Seams the product does not own.** The δάκος push depends on the grower installing the PWA and allowing notifications. iPhones only allow web push once the app is added to the home screen. The PDF's value depends on OPEKEPE and the accountant accepting it. Mill data is typed in by hand. Three names (OliveIQ, OliveLog, ΕλαιοLog) and a domain that is only planned make the first impression feel unfinished.

- Walk me through the first five minutes on a phone in the grove. When does the grower get their first useful result?
- Has an accountant or an OPEKEPE submission actually accepted the PDF? What did they change?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick the one thing and say it in one sentence.** The likely candidates are the paperwork-ready logbook or the δάκος alert. Put satellite, irrigation, traceability and mills on hold. Hide anything that does not serve the one thing during onboarding.
2. **Redesign the first session around a result the grower gets the same day:** an alert set up for their grove, or a logbook started from a template with a sample PDF. Show the retention number before and after the change.
3. **Close the seams:** one name and a live domain, a guided step for installing the app and allowing notifications, and written confirmation from at least a few accountants that the PDF works for them.
