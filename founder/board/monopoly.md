# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **A small, definable market to start in.** Greek-language, olive-only, small family groves is narrow enough to dominate, and generic farm-management software built in English for row crops leaves it to others.
- **A compliance hook.** The ΟΣΔΕ/OPEKEPE-ready field logbook ties the product to paperwork growers must produce every year anyway. Recurring duty is a better base than "nice to know".
- **A plausible path to network effects.** The five-year vision (every grove with a digital record, traceability from tree to shelf) and the mills already mentioned in the brand doc are the one route here to something a copycat cannot clone: data and relationships that grow with each grower and mill.
- **Already built.** Billing, push alerts, the demo and funnel analytics exist, so learning can start now and needs no more build.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No distribution.** Acquisition channels are undecided, the brief has no user numbers, and the buyer is an older, rural, low-software grower. A product nobody reaches is not a business. The cost to win one grower is unknown, and at €49 a year it has to be very low.
2. **No proprietary advantage today.** A degree-day pest model, OpenWeatherMap data, an OpenAI wrapper and a PDF export are each incremental and copyable by month six, whether by a cooperative, an agronomy supplier, an accounting firm or a funded agtech player. None of it is ten times better than the free substitutes: a notebook, the agronomist, the mill's own records.
3. **The wedge was cut away.** The MILL tier, the one thing that could create network effects and a cheap channel (one mill reaching dozens of growers), was collapsed into a consumer-style Pro tier. Selling grove by grove to individuals is the slowest, most competed route.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who else sells a Greek olive logbook or ΟΣΔΕ-ready record to this grower today? (/founder-competitors)
- How do the first 100 paying growers arrive, and what does each cost? (/founder-consumer, then /founder-cfo)
- How many Greek olive growers exist, how many file ΟΣΔΕ paperwork, and through whom? (/founder-competitors)
- What do you know about growers, mills or OPEKEPE that others do not? What is the secret?
- What does a grower lose by leaving after year three? If the answer is "nothing", there is no durability.
- Why now? Is a regulation, digital-subsidy push or traceability requirement forcing this record to exist?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick the beachhead and name it**: one region, or a handful of mills or cooperatives, where the founder can plausibly get a large share within a year.
2. **Rebuild the mill/cooperative channel** as the distribution engine and the source of shared data. Show at least one mill willing to onboard its growers.
3. **Show a real moat on the roadmap**: accumulated grove history, mill-to-grower traceability links, or an official compliance standing that a copycat could not match in six months.
4. **Bring the numbers back**: competitor map, acquisition cost and free-to-paid conversion from real users. Without them this lens moves to PASS.
