OliveIQ, a new Greek-language app for olive groves, on the phone or computer (oliveiq.gr). No download from an app store needed.
Free: one grove, a field diary for jobs and costs, harvests and mill deliveries, local weather.
Pro, €49 a year (or €6 a month): unlimited groves, δάκος risk alerts on your phone based on local weather, cost per litre of oil, an AI agronomist that answers in Greek, and a PDF field diary (Ημερολόγιο Αγρού) ready for ΟΣΔΕ or your accountant.
Made by an independent developer in Greece. Cancel any time.
